Overview

SHOPNAME is a locally owned trading card retailer. They sell thousands of different products, most being single cards without a traditional SKU and with fluctuating pricing. Traditional retail products may have less than a dozen on hand at a time, while SHOPNAME may carry thousands of a single card at a time. Additionally, they support the local gaming community of approximately 1000 individuals; Tracking customer trends is vital to better recommend products and to encourage brand loyalty. They average between 100-500 transactions per day, and managing their complex inventory is critical for both fulfilling customer orders and preserving client experience. They average between 100-500 transactions per day. SHOPNAME needs an efficient database to record their current inventory, track sales, and trace customer information.

Database description
Products
	Details the specifications of products that the business may carry.
	Productid: int, auto_increment, unique, not NULL, PK
	Name: varchar(255), not Null
	Price: decimal(12,2), not Null
	Relationships:
	M:N relationship with Inventories. Implemented using a junction table, Products-Inventories. ProductID is a foreign key inside this table.
Inventories:
	Details the different locations that the business has items at. For example, items in the display cases, stockroom, or in transit.
	InventoryID: int, auto_increment, unique, not NULL, PK
	Name: varchar(20), not NULL
	atStore: BOOL, not NULL
	M:N relationship with Products. Implemented using a junction table, Products-Inventories. InventoryID is a foreign key inside this table
	M:N relationship with Sales. Implemented with a junction table, Sales-Inventories. InventoryID is a foreign key inside this table. 
Customers:
	Details specifics on SHOPNAMES customers.
	customerID: int, auto_increment, unique, not NULL, PK
	lastName: varchar(30), not NULL
	firstName: varchar(30), not NULL
Email: varchar(80)
	Phone: varchar(15)
	lastPurchase: datetime, default -> datetime that entity was created
	points: int, not NULL, default -> 0
	1:M relationship with Orders. Implemented by including customerID as a foreign key inside of Orders
Orders:
	Details every transaction that the store makes.
	orderID: int, auto_increment, unique, not NULL, PK
	date: datetime, not NULL, default -> Current datetime
sequence: int, auto_increment, not NULL. Resets the counter to 0 at the start of each business day.
	customerID: int, FK
	amount: decimal(12,2), not NULL
	pointsSpent: int
	paid: decimal(12,2), 
	M:N relationship with Inventories. Implemented with a junction table Orders-Inventories. orderID is a foreign key inside the table
	M:1 relationship with Customers. Implemented by including customerID here as a foreign key
